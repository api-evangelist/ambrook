A pivotal scene in the 2011 movie Moneyball ends with the punchline, “How can you not be romantic about baseball?” The moral was that while baseball was moving towards statistics and quantitative analysis, everything still came down to people. For all the averages and benchmarks, on any given at bat a human drama was unfolding. Over my two years at Ambrook, that’s how I have come to feel about accounting.
