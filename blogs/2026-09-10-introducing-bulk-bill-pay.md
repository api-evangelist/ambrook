Now in Ambrook, it’s even easier to stay on top of your bills, whether you’re paying all of the week’s bills in one sitting or mailing one check for a month’s worth of invoices from the same vendor. Bulk bill pay is built around how vendors actually invoice you. How it works Getting started with bulk bill pay in Ambrook is simple: from the Bills tab, select any open bills you want to pay, across as many vendors as you like, and click Pay.
