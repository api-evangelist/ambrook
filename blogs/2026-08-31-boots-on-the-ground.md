Late last fall, a small group of Ambrook teammates headed to northern New England to beta test a new idea: Onboarding Week. The concept was simple—pair a group of new hires with a couple of seasoned company veterans for a week-long visit with an Ambrook customer. We visited Bread & Butter , a diversified livestock and produce farm in Vermont, and got a crash course in picking spinach and balancing books .
