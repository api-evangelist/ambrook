Show me a company blog — especially an engineering blog — and I’ll show you an enthusiastic batch of posts followed by the soul-sucking emptiness as the initial ideas dry up, motivation wanes, and the blog runs dry. It’s unclear whether the company still has an engineering team or if they just aren’t doing anything interesting anymore. Blogs generally have overestimated reach and underestimated cost.
