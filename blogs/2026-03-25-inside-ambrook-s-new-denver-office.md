The Ambrook Colorado team started out nomadic: work from home, then coffee shops, then larger and larger coworking spaces every several months. Now, a year after announcing our original Denver office , we’ve moved into a new space with room to work, host, and grow. Hello for the first time from Capitol Hill’s iconic 1904 schoolhouse on the National Register of Historic Places, right next to the Denver Art Museum and Central Library.
