At Ambrook, we build financial management tools for independent businesses. And in our world, “independent” has often meant multi-generational: We work with a sawmill operator who brought on his son and son-in-law as partners, a sixth-generation producer who stewards land that’s been in his family for more than a century, a father-daughter duo who raise lamb together. This means we can’t only focus on building tools that work well today or helping operators become profitable right now.
