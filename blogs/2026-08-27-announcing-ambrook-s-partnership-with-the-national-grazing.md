Ambrook’s mission is to build a more prosperous and resilient future for America’s family-run businesses. We’re starting with accounting, payments and cash management tools to help businesses manage finances, improve margins, and stay independent. The mission of the National Grazing Lands Coalition is to deliver assistance to grazing land stewards to foster the benefits grazing lands provide to family farms, ranches, local communities and all of society.
