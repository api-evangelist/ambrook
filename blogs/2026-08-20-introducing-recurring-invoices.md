With just a few clicks, you can now set any invoice in Ambrook to automatically repeat on a schedule of your choice. Once set up, Ambrook handles it from there: generating drafts or sending invoices, reminding customers of outstanding invoices, and collecting payments by card, ACH, and check, all automatically. Now, there’s no need to set a reminder or return to Ambrook to send monthly invoices.
