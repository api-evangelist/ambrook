In college, my design professor scolded a classmate for taking a marketing course. I always imagined he felt he needed to prepare us for a lifelong battle where we were trained to uphold creativity and novel thought in opposition to the cold hard numbers people. So when in Spring of 2024, I was tasked with co-leading our paid ads strategy for Meta, I couldn’t help but worry I’d feel like the sellout my professor warned me about.
