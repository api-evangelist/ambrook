# Ambrook

> Ambrook is financial management software for farms, ranches, construction, trucking, manufacturing, and the interconnected businesses that produce, build, and move the physical things our communities depend on. We combine bookkeeping, invoicing, payments, and inventory in one platform—with Schedule F/C tax alignment, enterprise tagging for tracking profitability by business line, and cost-per-head tracking for livestock operations. A QuickBooks alternative built for agriculture and industries where margins are tight and operations are complex.
